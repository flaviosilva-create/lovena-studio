Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This page describes general privacy considerations for Lovena Journal, a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. The final policy must be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual services, analytics, advertising tools and data practices enabled on the site before monetization is activated.

Information you provide

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry. We use it to respond and handle the request. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs as described in its own policies. Please consult GitHub’s privacy statement for information about its processing.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This publication may add analytics, advertising or consent-management tools in the future. Those services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure site use, deliver ads, prevent fraud or personalize advertising, depending on configuration and applicable law. This policy must be updated before those services are enabled to identify the actual providers and explain the available choices.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. We will not claim that a particular tool is active unless it has actually been implemented.

Choices and questions

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information. To ask a privacy question, contact support@lovenastudio.com. We will review the request and respond as appropriate.

Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site’s features or legal requirements change. The date at the top should be revised when material changes are made.

Important before monetization: This is a launch-stage draft, not legal advice. Before enabling AdSense or analytics, replace the conditional wording with a complete description of the actual services used and configure any required consent mechanism for the regions you serve.
