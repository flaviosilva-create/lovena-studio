Privacy Policy

How Lovena Journal handles information when you visit this publication or contact us.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy applies to the Lovena Journal website at https://flaviosilva-create.github.io/lovena-studio/blog/. It describes the website’s current, launch-stage features. It should be updated before additional services such as advertising, analytics, embedded media or newsletter tools are enabled.

Information you send us

The Journal does not currently provide visitor accounts, comment forms or newsletter sign-up forms. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your enquiry, review feedback or handle a correction request. Please do not send payment card details, passwords or other sensitive information by email.

Hosting and technical requests

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. When a browser requests a page or file, GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, requested resources and server logs to deliver, secure and operate the hosting service. Please consult GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details about its handling of information.

Fonts and images from third parties

The design may request font files from Google Fonts and image files from the Unsplash image CDN. When these resources load, the relevant provider may receive technical request information, such as an IP address and browser details. Their own privacy notices and terms govern their services. We do not control those providers’ systems.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

At the time of this update, the Lovena Journal pages do not contain Google AdSense advertising or an analytics tracking tag. No sign-up or comment form is active. If advertising, analytics, affiliate measurement or other tracking technologies are introduced, this policy will be revised to identify the relevant services, explain their purposes and describe the choices available to visitors. Any consent mechanism required for the regions served will be configured before those tools are activated.

Links to other websites

Articles and navigation may link to external websites. When you follow an external link, that website’s privacy policy and practices apply. We encourage readers to review the terms of third-party services they choose to use.

Your questions and requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information. To ask a privacy question or make a request concerning information you sent directly to us, contact support@lovenastudio.com. We may need enough information to understand and verify the request.

Changes to this policy

This notice may change as the Journal adds features or as legal requirements evolve. The update date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

Before monetization: This policy describes the current build and is not legal advice. It must be reviewed again against the actual AdSense, consent-management, analytics and other services configured before advertising is switched on.
