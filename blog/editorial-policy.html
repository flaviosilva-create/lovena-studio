Our standards

Editorial Policy

Lovena Journal aims to publish practical, original and reader-focused articles about gifts, personal writing and meaningful celebrations. These standards describe the process we intend to follow as the publication grows.

Research and writing

Articles are planned around a reader question and should provide useful steps, examples or decision-making guidance. We do not intentionally copy another publisher’s writing or publish pages solely to target keyword variations. When an article relies on outside factual claims, those claims should be checked against reliable sources and linked where appropriate.

Use of AI and editing tools

We may use AI-assisted tools to brainstorm, organize, draft or edit material. A human editorial review is responsible for assessing whether the final page is clear, useful, original and appropriate to publish. AI-generated text can be wrong, incomplete or overly generic, so it should not be treated as automatically verified.

Accuracy and corrections

If a reader identifies an error, please email support@lovenastudio.com and include the article URL and the detail in question. We will review the report and, when a correction is warranted, update the article. Material updates may be reflected in the article itself.

Recommendations and commercial relationships

Articles should prioritize the reader’s needs. If affiliate links, sponsorships or other paid relationships are introduced in the future, they should be disclosed clearly where required. At launch, do not assume that every product or service mentioned is independently tested or endorsed.

Advertising

If advertising is added, it should be clearly distinguishable from editorial content and must not interfere with navigation or mislead readers. We will not ask readers to click ads or use placements designed to create accidental clicks.

Contact

Send editorial questions and correction requests to support@lovenastudio.com.
