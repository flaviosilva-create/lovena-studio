Our standards

Editorial Policy

A clear account of the principles behind the stories and guides published by Lovena Journal.

Our editorial purpose

Lovena Journal publishes practical, reader-focused articles about thoughtful gifts, personal writing, relationship milestones and meaningful celebrations. Each article should answer a real reader question and provide useful context, examples or steps the reader can adapt.

Research and accuracy

We aim to verify factual claims against reliable sources before publication, especially when information can change or may affect a reader’s decision. When outside information materially supports an article, relevant sources should be linked. We avoid unsupported guarantees and distinguish general suggestions from professional advice.

Use of artificial intelligence

AI-assisted tools may be used to brainstorm, outline, draft, summarize research or improve language. These tools can produce incomplete, inaccurate or repetitive material. The publisher is responsible for reviewing the final article for originality, reader value, clarity, factual care and consistency with this policy. AI output is not published automatically simply because it has been generated.

Originality and updates

Articles are written for the Journal’s readers. We do not intentionally copy another publisher’s text, create near-duplicate pages for keyword variations or publish large volumes of low-value material. Pages may be updated when information becomes outdated, a meaningful improvement is needed or a correction is warranted.

Corrections and reader feedback

Readers may report an error by emailing support@lovenastudio.com with the article URL and a description of the issue. Reports are reviewed and, when a correction is appropriate, the content is updated. Significant changes may be reflected in the article’s update information.

Commercial relationships and recommendations

Editorial recommendations should be relevant to the topic and useful to readers. If sponsored content, affiliate links or other paid relationships are introduced, the relationship will be disclosed clearly and near the relevant content, as required by applicable rules. Readers should not assume that a product is independently tested unless an article explicitly says so and explains the basis.

Advertising standards

Advertising, when introduced, must remain distinguishable from editorial content. We will not ask readers to click ads, disguise ads as navigation or use placements intended to encourage accidental clicks. The Journal should remain useful and navigable even when advertising is present.

Questions about these standards?
Contact support@lovenastudio.com.
